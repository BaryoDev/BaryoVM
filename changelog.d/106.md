- **Source ranges for the SSH and web ports.** `vm harden --ssh-from <cidr,...>` limits port 22 in firewalld or ufw and refuses a list that excludes the address you are connecting from. `vm provision` takes per-port source ranges for the Lightsail firewall, including a `cloudflare` preset fetched at run time; a failed fetch aborts instead of opening the port. Unset ports stay open to `0.0.0.0/0` as before. ([#106])
