- **`stack rollback` goes back to the previous images.** `stack release` and `stack update` save the running images in `images-before.json` next to the backups before the first start. `stack rollback <name>` retags them and brings the stack up, and `--list` shows what is recorded. It never touches the database: it prints the matching backups and leaves `stack restore --yes` as the explicit step. ([#37])
