**`vm prune <name>`.** Plans disk cleanup by default (item, size, reason) and deletes nothing until `--apply`, which prints the space freed and `df` before and after. Never touches named volumes, images any container uses, `bin/` without a project file, or dirty worktrees. `--floor <GB>` does nothing while enough space is free, and apply takes a `flock` on the VM.
